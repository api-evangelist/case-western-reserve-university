Apply for a Sears think[box] Student Project Fund Hero Type Image Katie Laux Thu, 10/15/2026 - 15:18 Student Life Editorial Staff October 16, 2026 Sears think[box] at Case Western Reserve University invites students to apply for the Student Project Fund.The Student Project Fund provides up to $2,500 to help students bring extracurricular project ideas to life. Funding may be used for materials, equipment, manufacturing costs and other project expenses. Food, travel and stipends are not eligible for funding.Students may apply individually or as part of a group or team.
