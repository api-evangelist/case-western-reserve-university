CWRU recognized with 2026 Fiesta of Hope Partner Award Hero Type Image Brianna Smith Thu, 10/01/2026 - 16:29 Awards Editorial Staff October 02, 2026 During Hispanic Heritage Month, observed annually from Sept. 15 to Oct. 15, Case Western Reserve University received the 2026 Fiesta of Hope Partner Award from Esperanza.The award recognizes CWRU’s long-standing collaboration with Esperanza and its commitment to supporting student success through connections with community organizations across Cleveland.
